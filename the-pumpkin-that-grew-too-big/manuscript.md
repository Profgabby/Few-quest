# The Pumpkin That Grew Too Big

**Collection:** GardenVerse · **Tier:** Little Explorers · **Language:** English
**Editorial status:** Draft for review

## Scene 1: One Small Pumpkin

Bola planted pumpkin seeds with Grandpa near the sunny edge of the garden. The vines spread over the soil, and wide leaves made patches of shade. One day, Bola found a little green pumpkin tucked beneath a leaf. It was no bigger than her two hands together. ‘This one is mine to watch,’ she said. Grandpa tied a soft tag to a nearby stake so they could find it again. ‘It belongs to the plant,’ he reminded her, ‘but you can help care for it.’

## Scene 2: A Little Bigger

Each week, Bola returned to the same pumpkin. First it was as round as a small ball. Then it was wider than her lunch plate. She measured around it with a piece of string and marked the length on a chart. ‘It keeps growing!’ she said. Grandpa checked that the vine had room to spread and that the soil was not too dry. Bola gave the plant space and tried not to step on its long stems. She wondered how big the pumpkin might become.

**Illustration brief:** Bola measuring circumference of growing pumpkin with soft string; Grandpa checks vine.

## Scene 3: The Path Disappears

One morning, Bola came with a basket of garden tools. She stopped at the narrow path. The pumpkin vine had crept across it, and the growing fruit rested near the middle. ‘How will we get through?’ she asked. Grandpa looked at the long vine. ‘We should not yank it,’ he said. ‘That could damage the plant.’ They walked around the bed while they thought. Bola wanted to move the pumpkin, but it already felt much heavier than before.

## Scene 4: A Clever Detour

Bola fetched two flat stepping stones from the shed. Grandpa helped her place them on firm ground beside the garden bed, away from the vine. They made a little detour so everyone could pass without stepping on leaves or fruit. Bola tested the stones while Grandpa watched. They were steady. ‘The pumpkin can stay where it is,’ she said, ‘and we can still reach the beans.’ Grandpa smiled. ‘That is a good solution. You thought about the plant and the people.’

**Illustration brief:** Bola and Grandpa placing two stable stepping stones around vine, not on plant.

## Scene 5: Orange as Sunset

More days passed. The pumpkin’s skin changed from green to a rich orange. Its stalk became firm, and the vine around it began to dry. Grandpa checked the fruit carefully. ‘It may be ready soon,’ he said. Bola wanted to lift it at once, but Grandpa reminded her that a heavy pumpkin could hurt her back or toes if it fell. ‘We will harvest it together,’ he promised. Bola took one last measurement and drew a bright orange circle on her chart.

**Illustration brief:** Mature orange pumpkin on drying vine, Bola sketching, Grandpa assessing ripeness.

2. Why did Grandpa say to wait for help?

## Scene 6: The Big Lift

On harvest day, Grandpa used a clean garden tool to cut the stalk, leaving a short handle. He and another adult lifted the heavy pumpkin onto a sturdy cart. Bola walked beside them, carrying her chart. At the table, they weighed the pumpkin together. Bola was amazed by the number on the scale, but Grandpa did not ask her to carry the fruit. ‘You cared for it while it grew,’ he said. ‘That was your important job.’ Bola felt taller than the pumpkin itself.

## Scene 7: Enough to Share

Grandpa cut the pumpkin safely in the kitchen while Bola watched from a clear space. Inside were pale seeds and bright flesh. Some pieces would become soup, and others would be roasted for the family meal. Grandpa saved a few healthy seeds for another planting season. Bola drew the final page of her chart: one small pumpkin, one enormous pumpkin, and a family table. ‘It grew too big for our path,’ she said, ‘but just right for sharing.’ Everyone laughed, and Bola asked when they could plant again.
