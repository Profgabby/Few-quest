# The Little Seed That Would Not Wake

**Collection:** GardenVerse · **Tier:** Little Explorers · **Language:** English
**Editorial status:** Draft for review

## Scene 1: A Bed for a Seed

Mimi held a tiny brown seed on her palm. It was smaller than the button on her yellow shirt. Grandma showed her a sunny corner of the garden. Together they loosened the soil and made a shallow hole. Mimi tucked the seed inside and covered it gently. ‘Good morning, little seed,’ she whispered, although it was nearly lunchtime. She set a small wooden marker beside the spot so she would remember where she had planted it.

## Scene 2: Nothing Yet

The next morning, Mimi ran outside before breakfast. She looked beside her wooden marker. There was only brown soil. She looked again after breakfast. Still nothing. ‘Perhaps my seed is too sleepy,’ she said. Grandma smiled. ‘A seed can be busy even when we cannot see it.’ Mimi pressed her ear close to the ground. She heard a bird singing and a bicycle passing, but no tiny seed. She wondered what might be happening underneath.

## Scene 3: A Gentle Drink

Mimi carried a little watering can to the bed. She wanted to pour every drop in one big splash. Grandma put a hand on the handle. ‘Slowly,’ she said. ‘The soil should be moist, not flooded.’ Mimi tipped the can until a soft shower darkened the earth. Then she stopped. She touched the soil with one finger. It felt damp and cool. ‘There,’ she said. ‘A drink for someone I cannot see.’ She left the seed to rest in its quiet place.

## Scene 4: Waiting Is Hard

Two days passed. Mimi watered only when the soil began to feel dry. She watched a butterfly rest on a leaf and ants carry crumbs across the path. But beside her marker, nothing green appeared. ‘I am tired of waiting,’ she told Grandma. Grandma brought two stools into the shade. ‘Some good things take time,’ she said. They sat together and counted birds. Mimi still wished the seed would hurry, but sitting with Grandma made the waiting easier.

## Scene 5: A Tiny Hook

After a night of light rain, Mimi went to check the marker. Something had changed. A small green hook pushed through the soil. Mimi bent down so quickly that her hat slipped over one eye. ‘Grandma! It woke up!’ she called. Grandma came outside and knelt beside her. ‘That is a new shoot,’ she said. ‘The seed has started growing.’ Mimi wanted to pull the shoot higher to help it. Instead, she folded her hands and let the little plant grow by itself.

## Scene 6: Two Little Leaves

Each morning, Mimi looked for a change. The green hook straightened. Then two small leaves opened like tiny hands. Mimi made a drawing in her notebook. On the first page she drew the brown seed. On the next, she drew the shoot. On the third, she drew the two leaves. ‘Now I can see part of its story,’ she told Grandma. ‘And there is more to come,’ Grandma answered. Mimi placed the notebook safely on the shelf before going outside to look again.

## Scene 7: Good Morning, Little Plant

One bright morning, Mimi stood beside the bed with her watering can. The little plant was taller now, but it was still small enough to fit beneath her hand. She gave the soil a careful drink and checked that no one had stepped on the bed. ‘I thought you were asleep,’ she told the plant. ‘You were growing where I could not see.’ Grandma smiled from the path. Mimi smiled too. Tomorrow might bring another leaf. She was happy to wait and find out.
